## Your lines
**Hook (0:00):** "In most cities, when 10 people report the same burst pipe, the city opens 10 tickets.

close that gap."

**Demo narration:** follow `docs/DEMO.md`. Use v1 golden for the naive-vs-CONVERGE table; use v2 root cause for the hypothesis and crew-visit moment (steps 4 and 5).

**Close:** "Same reports, fewer work orders, zero false closures accepted, every decision auditable.
CONVERGE is the verification layer every city complaint system is missing."

## Judge questions you take
- *What's novel?* The verified loop: closure needs evidence, contradictions reopen, and root causes
  merge repairs (v2). Most tools stop at classification or dashboards.
- *Why not just use AI for everything?* Rules decide so decisions are explainable and auditable; AI
  explains in plain language and is capped so it can't change an outcome.
- *How would a city adopt it?* It sits on top of their existing complaint portal via API; pilot one
  ward, one category.
