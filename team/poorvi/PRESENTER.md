# Poorvi — market gap, results, business, future

## You own
- Why existing systems fall short, the real user-testing results, business model, future.

## Your lines
**Market gap (0:45):** "India's CPGRAMS, the Swachhata app, FixMyStreet, SeeClickFix: they collect
complaints and route them. Some group nearby duplicates, but none of them link *connected* problems
to a shared cause or refuse to close a case until evidence proves it's fixed. That's the gap."
(Safer wording: SeeClickFix advertises duplicate detection. See `research.md`.)

**Results (4:15):** read the numbers from the dashboard's metrics and the feedback panel. Use only
what's on screen: "N people tested it, average rating X out of 5".
**Business:** "Sold to city corporations and command-and-control centres as a verification layer
over their existing complaint portal, priced per ward or per department."
**Future:** "The same engine works for water networks, food redistribution and healthcare admin.
One loop: report, link, act, verify."

- *Competitors?* See `team/poorvi/research.md` (the coordinator will draft it for you to read).
