Smart City App – Key Features

1. Interactive City Map – Visually locate roads, public places, infrastructure, and reported issues on an interactive map.
2. Specific Area Selection – Select a particular road, district, locality, or incident location to report or track issues.
3. AI-Based Complaint Prioritization – Automatically prioritize critical and emergency complaints over routine issues based on severity, urgency, and public impact.
4. Traffic Intelligence – Monitor traffic conditions, identify congestion, suggest alternative routes, and provide real-time traffic updates.
5. Environment & Air Quality Monitoring – Track AQI, pollution levels, noise pollution, and other environmental conditions across different areas.
6. AI City Assistant – An AI-powered assistant that answers citizens’ questions, helps report complaints, provides city information, and guides users to relevant civic services.
